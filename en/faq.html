Help

Last updated: 2026-10-17

How do we connect?

Go to Settings → Connect with your person, make an invitation link and send it. They open it on their iPhone, install Kokomado and add a nickname.

The link won't open in my chat app

Choose "Open in Safari" from the app's menu.

What does my person need?

An iPhone signed in to iCloud, with iOS 18 or later.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold an empty spot on the home screen → Edit → Add Widget → search for Kokomado.

How fast do cards arrive?

Usually within seconds to minutes. Low Power Mode or a weak connection can delay it; opening the app refreshes right away.

How many cards per day?

One per person per day. You can redo today's card; it replaces the earlier one.

Does Premium cover us both?

Yes. One purchase covers both connected people.

What about a new iPhone?

Your cards are in iCloud. Sign in with the same Apple Account and they come back.
