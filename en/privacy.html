Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-17

1. What we collect

Kokomado needs no account. The developer collects no personal information — no names, emails, location or usage data — and uses no ads or third-party analytics.

2. Where your data lives

Cards, nicknames and dates are stored only on your iPhone and in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit). The developer cannot see them.

3. Sharing with your person

Once you connect with an invitation link, you both view and add the same cards through Apple's iCloud sharing. Shared data is stored in the inviter's iCloud. The inviter can stop sharing and the invitee can disconnect at any time.

4. Camera and photos

Accessed only when you take or choose a photo; it becomes part of a card saved in iCloud.

5. Notifications

When you leave a card, answer or poke, the app sends a notification request to Kokomado's push relay (running on Cloudflare), which hands it to Apple's push service for your person's iPhone. The request contains only their device's push token, your nickname, the short text shown in the notification (the card's caption, an emoji or the poke's words) and where the card is in iCloud — never the card picture. The relay only passes it on and stores nothing. Their iPhone reads the card picture straight from iCloud. The evening reminder and date reminders are created on your iPhone.

6. Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. The developer never receives your payment details.

7. Deleting data

Settings → Delete all data removes everything.
